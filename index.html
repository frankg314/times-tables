How to play

Each turn, move one clip to a new number. Multiply the two clip numbers, then tap the answer on the board to claim it. If you tap the wrong square, you miss your turn. Get four squares in a row (across, down or diagonal) before Robo does.

Think ahead: which answers can you make next turn, and which ones are you leaving for Robo?
